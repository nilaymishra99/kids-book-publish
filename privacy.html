Privacy policy

How Little Legends collects, uses and protects the information and photos you give us.

Last updated: [Date]

1. What we collect

  • The child's first name, age and the pronouns you choose (no date of birth is needed).
  • Photos of the child and anyone else you add to the book, uploaded by you.
  • Names and relationships of the people in the book, and any dedication message you write.
  • Your name, email and, for printed orders, phone number and delivery address, collected by our payment provider at checkout.
  • Basic order details, such as the format you chose, the payment status and an order reference.

2. How we use it

  • To create the illustrated book, including sending photos to our image provider to draw the pictures.
  • To take payment, deliver the book and respond to your questions.
  • To prevent fraud and meet our legal and tax obligations.
  • To improve the site, using aggregated information that does not identify you.

3. Where photos are kept

  • Your preview stays in the browser on your own device. We don't keep a copy on our servers.
  • Photos are sent to our AI image provider only to create your illustrations. [Confirm the provider's terms: no training on API inputs, and the retention period.]
  • For printed books, the finished pages are kept in private cloud storage until the order has shipped and then deleted. [Set the exact number of days, for example 30.]
  • Order records, such as the name, email and payment status, are kept for [X years] for accounting and tax purposes.

4. Who we share it with

  • Our payment provider (Stripe), to take payment.
  • Our AI image provider, to draw the illustrations.
  • Our cloud hosting and storage provider, to run the site and hold print files.
  • Our print and courier partner, [name], once you've placed a printed order.
  • Our email provider, to send order confirmations.

5. We don't sell your data

We never sell your personal information or your children's photos, and we don't use them for advertising. We only share information with the providers listed above, and only as needed to run the service.

6. Children

Little Legends is for parents and guardians. You must be an adult, and the child's parent or legal guardian, to upload photos. Everyone pictured should have agreed to their photo being used, or had a guardian agree on their behalf. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Ask us what information we hold about you or your child.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it, including photos, at any time by emailing [privacy@example.com].
  • Withdraw consent for future use. This may mean we can no longer make or deliver the book.
  • Complain to the data protection authority in your country.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, private storage for photos and files, and limit access to people who need it. No online service is perfectly secure, so please tell us straight away if you think something has gone wrong.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process information outside your country, including in India and the United States. Where this happens we rely on their contractual safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make a significant change, we'll tell you on the site or by email before it takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email [privacy@example.com] or see our contact page.
