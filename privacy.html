Our privacy promise

Plain-English summary. Have this reviewed by a lawyer before launch (see README).

What we collect

The photos you upload, the names you type, your child's age and the dedication message. When you order, our payment provider (Stripe) collects your name, email, payment details and — for printed books — your delivery address and phone number.

How photos are used

  • Your preview stays on your device. The book you build is stored in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • Illustration. To draw your comic, each photo is sent securely to our AI illustration provider for that one request. We don't store it, and we use a provider that doesn't train its models on customer images sent through its API.
  • Printing. For printed books only, the finished pages (not the original photos) are uploaded after payment to private storage so we can print them. We delete them within 30 days of your order shipping.
  • We never sell photos, share them with advertisers, use them for marketing, or use them to train AI.

Consent

You must be the child's parent or legal guardian, and have permission from every other person pictured (or their guardian), before uploading photos.

Deleting your data

Clearing your browser's site data removes your saved previews. To have print files or order details deleted sooner, email us with your order reference and we'll confirm once it's done.
