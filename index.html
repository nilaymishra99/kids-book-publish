Questions parents ask

What kind of photo works best?

A clear, well-lit, front-facing photo where the face fills most of the picture. No sunglasses or hats, and one person per photo. You can zoom and position each photo before we use it.

Who can be in the book?

Your child is always the hero. You can add up to five more people — parents, siblings, grandparents, cousins, friends — or a pet. If you don't add anyone, the story's own sidekick keeps your child company.

Which age group should I choose?

Tiny Tots (0–2) has one big picture and a few words per page. Little Explorers (3–5) are short read-aloud adventures. Young Heroes (6–8) and Super Readers (9–10) are full comic layouts with more to read.

Can I see the book before paying?

Yes — you see every single page as a watermarked preview first. The watermark disappears as soon as you order.

How long does delivery take?

Digital comics are ready the moment you pay. Printed books ship within 5–7 working days.

Is it okay to upload photos of my children?

Only upload photos you have the right to use — you'll be asked to confirm you're the parent or guardian and that everyone pictured is happy to appear. See our privacy promise for exactly how photos are handled.
