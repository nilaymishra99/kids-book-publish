Refund and replacement policy

Because every book is personalised and printed to order, this policy explains when you're entitled to a replacement or refund.

Last updated: [Date]

Damaged, defective or misprinted books

If your book arrives damaged, defective or misprinted, we'll send a free replacement or give you a full refund. Email hello@example.com within [14] days of delivery with your order reference and a photo of the problem.

When refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within [7] working days. Some banks and card providers can take longer to show the money. If you haven't seen a refund after [14] working days, contact us with your order reference.

Personalised books: change of mind

Each book has your child's name and photos, and it's printed on demand. Once printing has started, we can't offer a refund for a change of mind. Please check the full preview carefully before you order a printed copy.

Cancelling before printing

If you cancel before printing starts, you'll get a full refund. Email us within [12 hours] of ordering, with your order reference. Once printing has started, the cancellation rules above apply.

Digital comics

Digital comics are delivered instantly, so they can't be returned once downloaded. If the PDF won't open or is faulty, email us and we'll fix it or refund you.

Need help?

Email hello@example.com with your order reference. We aim to reply within [24 hours].
