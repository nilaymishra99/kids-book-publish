Frequently asked questions

Everything about making, previewing, ordering and receiving your book.

What do I need to make a book?

Your child's first name, their age, and one clear photo of them. You can also add up to five family members, friends or a pet, each with a photo and a name in the story. Each person needs one photo.

What kind of photo works best?

A clear, well-lit, front-facing photo with one person in it, where the face fills most of the frame. Please avoid sunglasses, heavy shadows and group shots. You can zoom and position each photo before it's used.

Who can appear in the book?

Your child is always the hero. You can add up to five more people: parents, grandparents, siblings, cousins, friends or a pet. If you don't add anyone, the story's own sidekick keeps your child company.

Can I see the book before I pay?

Yes. Every page is shown as a watermarked preview before you order. The watermark is removed once your payment is confirmed.

Which age group should I choose?

Tiny Tots (0–2) has one big picture and a few words per page. Little Explorers (3–5) are short read-aloud adventures. Young Heroes (6–8) and Super Readers (9–10) have full comic layouts with more to read.

What is the difference between AI illustration and photo-comic style?

When AI illustration is on, every scene is drawn in your chosen art style using your photos as a guide to likeness. Photo-comic style turns your real photos into comic-style portraits inside illustrated scenes. Both use the same story and text.

What formats are available and how much do they cost?

Digital comic (₹699): an instant PDF you can print at home or read on any device. Softcover comic (₹1,499): a glossy 8×8 in softcover with the digital PDF included. Premium hardcover (₹2,299): a durable 8×8 in hardcover with a gift-ready finish and the digital PDF included.

Can I order more than one book?

Yes. Each book is a separate order, so start a new comic for each one.

Can I change the name or message after I pay?

Before you pay, you can change anything on the preview. After payment, contact us within [24 hours] if you need a change and we'll see what we can do before printing starts.

I started on my phone but want to pay on my laptop. Is that possible?

Your preview is saved in the browser where you made it. Please finish on the same device and browser. If you've paid on another device and can't see your book, email us with your order reference and we'll sort it out.

What happens to my photos?

Your photos are only used to create your book. They are never sold, shared for advertising or used to train AI. Read our privacy policy for the full details, including how long we keep things.

What if my book arrives damaged?

If your book arrives damaged, defective or misprinted, we'll send a free replacement or a full refund. Email us within [14] days of delivery with your order reference and a photo. See our refund policy for the details.

How long is a book, and what does extra length cost?

Every book is 15 pages as standard, and you can choose up to 30. Each page above 15 costs ₹200 on top of the format price, so a 20-page softcover is ₹2,899.

What is a bundle?

The bundle builder asks about your child's age, interests, format and book length. It then picks the three best-matching stories, makes all three with your child as the hero, and takes 20% off the total.
